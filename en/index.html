Give your long-waiting clothes an outing — and play some board games.

Dress code

Everything you usually have nowhere to wear. Too dressy, too strange, too bold for everyday life.

No need to buy anything: the whole idea is to take out what’s gathering dust, or to put together an unusual look from what you already have.

No outfit, or don’t feel like inventing one? Come in whatever is comfortable — the main thing is to have fun and relax.

What’s on

  • Board games. Every win earns you one Closet Coin.

  • Mini-market. Swap things for Closet Coins.

  • Dinner. I’m making chicken breast in pesto with rice and vegetables, and baked pears with a sweet cheese sauce for dessert.

What you can bring

(optional, only if you like)

  • snacks and drinks of your choice
  • something small for the mini-market — anything lying around unused
  • your favourite board gameswe already have Imaginarium, Deni, playing cards and Exploding Kittens
  • fairy lights and string lightsyou can never have too many
  • something warm: it may get chilly in the evening

If it rains, we’ll move indoors, and there’s less room there — so please do message me privately if you’re coming.
